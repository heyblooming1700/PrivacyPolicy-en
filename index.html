Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile and web-based AI Roleplay Application and associated platform services operated by the Service Provider (hereinafter referred to as the "Application" or "Service").

1. Information We Collect

To deliver seamless conversational roleplay experiences and maintain account security, the Application collects the following categories of data:

  • Essential Information: Account email address, display username, encrypted credentials, conversation session histories, transactional payment logs, and network IP addresses.
  • Automated Diagnostics: Service usage patterns, visited screens, operating system (OS) versions, and client application runtime metrics.

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application and integrated third-party development tools may use local storage, session cookies, and related tracking technologies to provide core functional support, maintain authentication state, and compile aggregated performance analytics. You can adjust your browser or device preferences to manage cookie permissions at any time.

3. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Data Processing

The Application utilizes generative large language models (LLMs) to power character dialogue and contextual interactions. Dialogue history and prompt inputs are securely processed through protected AI endpoints to provide personalized storytelling. All data processing is strictly handled under secure privacy standards and is never sold to unauthorized third parties.

4. Third-Party Services and Integrations

To improve software reliability, the Application integrates industry-standard third-party developer frameworks that process data according to their respective privacy guidelines:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob / Advertising Services

5. Data Retention and Deletion

The Service Provider retains personal information only for the duration necessary to fulfill service purposes or comply with statutory requirements:

  • Commercial Transaction Records: Retained for 5 years in compliance with applicable consumer protection and e-commerce laws.
  • Customer Dispute Resolution Records: Retained for 3 years to address inquiries and user rights disputes.
  • Access Logs and Diagnostic Telemetry: Retained for 3 months for security compliance and fraud prevention.
  • User-Generated Content and Account Data: Permanently purged without delay upon verified account termination requests.

6. User Privacy Rights

Users hold the right to inspect, correct, export, or permanently delete their personal information at any time. To withdraw consent or submit a formal data erasure request, please contact our support team at privacy@example.com.

7. Child Privacy Protection

The Application is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 14 (or applicable statutory age limit), and we do not knowingly solicit personal data from children without parental or legal guardian consent. If we become aware that underage data has been collected inadvertently, it will be deleted immediately. Legal guardians may submit inquiries regarding child data to privacy@example.com.

8. Security Safeguards

The Service Provider implements robust technical, physical, and administrative measures—including encrypted communications and role-based access management—to ensure high data protection. In the event of a security incident, affected users will be notified in accordance with relevant statutory obligations.

9. Revisions to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological adjustments or regulatory updates. Substantive changes will be announced via in-app notifications at least 7 days prior to their effective date.

10. Contact Information

If you have any questions, feedback, or grievance reports concerning data processing practices, please contact our Privacy Team:

  • Privacy Support: privacy@example.com
